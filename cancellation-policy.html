Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Passengers can ask the bus company responsible for their trip about cancellation. Wayline does not currently provide an in-app workflow for cancelling an individual reservation, so contact the company directly and wait for its confirmation. A company can cancel an entire trip in Wayline; when it does, all reservations for that trip are cancelled and seats are released. Wayline does not automatically notify passengers.

How to request a cancellation

  1. Contact the bus company listed on your booking using the contact details it provided.
  2. Include your booking reference, passenger name, and travel date so the booking can be identified.
  3. Wait for confirmation from the relevant bus company. Sending a request does not itself cancel the reservation, and an individual cancellation will not update the Wayline booking record.

Do not include your SMS verification code or payment credentials in a cancellation request.

When a booking is cancelled

Wayline continues to show an individual reservation as saved because individual cancellation is handled directly with the bus company and is not recorded in Wayline. If a company cancels a whole trip through Wayline, all its reservations are marked cancelled and the affected passengers should be contacted directly. Wayline does not collect payments, issue refunds, or determine refund eligibility. If you paid the bus company directly, that company decides whether a refund is due and handles it. Contact the company promptly with your booking reference to ask about its refund rules.

Payment status

Wayline currently records bookings as unpaid and does not process payments. Choosing MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money is not a payment and does not confirm that money was received.
