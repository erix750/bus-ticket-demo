Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Passengers can contact Wayline to request cancellation of a booking. Cancellation must be confirmed by the bus company responsible for the trip; sending a request does not itself cancel the reservation.

How to request a cancellation

  1. Call 0798230630 or email muvunyieric25@gmail.com.
  2. Include your booking reference, passenger name, and travel date so the booking can be identified.
  3. Wait for confirmation from the relevant bus company. Sending a request does not itself cancel the reservation.

Do not include your SMS verification code or payment credentials in a cancellation request.

When a booking is cancelled

Your reservation remains active until the bus company confirms the cancellation. Seat availability is updated only when the booking is marked cancelled. Wayline does not collect payments, issue refunds, or determine refund eligibility. If you paid the bus company directly, that company decides whether a refund is due and handles it. Contact the company promptly with your booking reference to ask about its refund rules.

Payment status

Wayline currently records bookings as unpaid and does not process payments. Choosing MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money is not a payment and does not confirm that money was received.

For more information, read the Terms of Service or contact Wayline.
