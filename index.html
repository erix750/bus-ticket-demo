    Frequently asked questions

    How do I book a bus?

    Book online by searching your route and travel date, then choosing an available bus and entering passenger details. USSD booking by dialing *200# is planned, but the code is not active until Africa’s Talking provisions it and the service is deployed. Your booking reference appears after a reservation is saved.

    Is my booking paid?

    No. MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money are currently recorded only as your intended payment method. Wayline does not collect or verify payment yet, so your booking is marked unpaid and is not proof of payment.

    How do I cancel a booking?

    Contact the bus company listed on your booking with its reference to request a cancellation. A reservation is not cancelled until the company confirms it. See our Cancellation Policy for details.

    Where can I find my booking reference?

    Your booking reference is shown after your booking is saved. Keep it available to find your booking or when contacting the bus company.

    Can I view my booking from another device?

    Yes. Enter the booking reference and the phone number used at checkout, then verify the one-time SMS code. No passenger password or account setup is needed.

    How can I contact Wayline?

    Official Wayline support contacts are not yet published. For booking questions or changes, contact the bus company listed on your reservation and include your booking reference.
