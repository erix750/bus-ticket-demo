Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Wayline provides bus schedule search and booking services in Rwanda. This policy explains what passenger information is used and why.

Information used for a booking

When you make a booking, Wayline uses the passenger name and phone number you provide, together with the selected route, travel date, bus, seat count, booking reference, and intended payment method. A passenger account is not required.

How your phone number is used

  • Your number is associated with the booking so the relevant bus company can handle that reservation and contact you about the trip.
  • To look up a booking on another device, you enter its reference and booking phone number and verify the number with a one-time SMS code. The code is used for verification, not as a password.
  • SMS verification is delivered through the authentication and messaging providers configured for Wayline. Those providers process information needed to deliver and verify the code.

Who can access booking information

Booking details are made available to the bus company responsible for the selected schedule so it can manage the reservation. They are not displayed as public passenger records. Wayline uses Supabase to store and process schedules, bookings, and authentication information. Service providers process information as needed to provide their services.

Payments, accounts, and device preferences

MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money are currently recorded only as your intended payment method. Wayline does not collect payment credentials or process or verify these payments. Company account sign-in and passenger phone verification use authentication services. The website also saves your light or dark display preference in your browser.

Storage and requests

Booking information is retained while it is needed to provide and support the booking, maintain business records, and meet applicable requirements. To ask a question or make a privacy request, contact Wayline. Requests may need to be reviewed with the relevant bus company and may be subject to records that must be retained for operational or legal reasons.
