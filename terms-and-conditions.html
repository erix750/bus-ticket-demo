USING WAYLINE

Terms of Service

Last updated: November 10, 2026

These terms apply when you use Wayline to view bus schedules or make a booking. Wayline provides an online booking service for bus trips in Rwanda.

Schedules and fares

Bus companies publish and operate their schedules. Check the route, travel date, departure time, passenger count, and fare shown before confirming a booking. Route distances may be estimates, and schedules, availability, and fares can change. The bus company is responsible for providing the trip it publishes.

Passenger details and booking

Provide a correct passenger name and a phone number you can access. The phone number and reservation details are shared with the relevant bus company to manage the booking. Keep your booking reference so you can look up the reservation or ask for help.

Payment status

Selecting MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money records only your intended payment method. Wayline does not currently initiate, collect, or verify payment. A saved booking is marked unpaid and is not proof of payment or a paid ticket. Confirm payment arrangements directly with the bus company.

Cancellations and acceptable use

To request a cancellation, contact Wayline with your booking reference. The reservation remains active until the relevant bus company confirms the cancellation. See the Cancellation Policy for details.

Use Wayline lawfully and provide accurate information. Do not misuse the website, interfere with its operation, or attempt to access another passenger’s booking or a company account without authorization.

Service availability and contact

Wayline aims to provide current booking information but does not guarantee uninterrupted access or that a third-party bus service will operate without change. If displayed schedule details appear incorrect, contact Wayline before travelling.
